1. Who we are

Ikhlaas (“Ikhlaas”, “we”, “us”) is an application-only matrimonial service for Muslims seeking marriage (nikah), operated by [LEGAL ENTITY]. This policy explains what personal data we collect through the Ikhlaas mobile app and related services, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. It is designed to align with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) and Google Play requirements.

By creating an account you consent to the processing described here.

2. Who can use Ikhlaas

Ikhlaas is only for adults 18 years or older who are seeking marriage. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to a minor, we delete it.

3. Information we collect

a. Account & contact

  • Your name and email address (via Google Sign-In or a one-time email code).
  • Your mobile phone number.

b. Identity verification (sensitive)

  • A selfie and a photo of a government-issued ID (e.g. passport or Aadhaar), used solely to confirm you are a real person.
  • ID images are stored in a restricted, admin-only area, are never shown to other members, and are deleted if your application is not accepted.

c. Profile & eligibility

  • Date of birth, gender, marital status, height/weight, city and country, nationality, languages, education, profession, and similar profile details.
  • Your responses to the eligibility questionnaire, including religious beliefs and practice (creed and finance affirmations, prayer, fasting, and related answers). This is special-category / sensitive data collected with your explicit consent to operate a faith-based matrimonial service.
  • Profile photos you upload.

d. Location & device

  • Approximate device location (latitude/longitude and accuracy) captured with your application, used for integrity of the pool and location-based matching.
  • Device details such as model, manufacturer, operating system, and app version.

e. Communications

  • Messages you exchange in guarded conversations. Messages are server-mediated and filtered (for example, sharing contact details before the family stage is blocked).
  • Guardian (Wali) details you provide — name, relationship, and phone number.

f. Usage & notifications

  • App activity needed to run matching, safety, and moderation (e.g. matches shown, interests expressed, reports).
  • A push-notification token to send you match, message, and request alerts.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To assess eligibility and curate faith-aligned matches.
  • To verify identity and keep the pool genuine and safe.
  • To enable guarded conversations and, on mutual agreement, the family stage.
  • To send a periodic conversation summary to a member’s guardian (Wali) where that member has chosen to involve one.
  • To moderate content, investigate reports, and enforce our rules.
  • To send service notifications and respond to support requests.

5. How your information is shared

WithWhat & why
Other members Your profile and photos are shown to potential matches subject to your privacy setting (photos can be blurred until a match, or hidden until you approve a request). Income, residency, and nationality are withheld from match cards and disclosed only after a mutual conversation opens.
Guardians (Wali) Where a member involves a guardian, that guardian may receive a summary of the member’s conversation activity, and — at the family stage, on mutual agreement — guardian contact details are exchanged between the two families.
Service providers Google Firebase (authentication, database, file storage, cloud functions, and push messaging), an email provider for one-time sign-in codes, and a messaging provider used to deliver guardian summaries. These providers process data on our behalf under their terms.
Legal & safety Where required by law, or to prevent harm, fraud, or abuse.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

6. Data retention

  • Government-ID images of applicants who are not accepted are deleted.
  • Your profile and conversation data are retained while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your login, profile, application, identity data, and photos are removed (some records may be retained where required for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes).

7. How we protect your information

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with access controls.
  • Government-ID images are kept in a restricted store unreachable by other members.
  • Photos served to members are processed server-side (permission-checked, blurred where applicable, and watermarked to trace misuse).
  • Sensitive actions are mediated by our servers rather than performed directly by the app.
