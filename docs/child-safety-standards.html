Child Safety Standards

Our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Last updated: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Zero tolerance. Ikhlaas has a strict zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Ikhlaas is an adults-only matrimonial service and is not directed at, or open to, anyone under 18.

1. Adults only (18+)

Ikhlaas is exclusively for adults aged 18 or older who are seeking marriage (nikah). Creating an account requires confirming date of birth and passing identity verification (a government-ID and selfie check). We do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to register. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we remove it immediately and preserve relevant information as required by law.

2. Prohibited conduct

The following are strictly forbidden on Ikhlaas and result in immediate removal and a permanent ban:

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM), or content that sexualises a minor.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any attempt to contact, exploit, or endanger a minor.
  • Sharing, requesting, or linking to CSAE/CSAM content.
  • Any use of the service by, or on behalf of, a person under 18.

3. How we prevent and detect abuse

  • Age & identity checks — date-of-birth confirmation plus a government-ID and selfie verification keep the community adult and genuine.
  • Guarded, server-mediated messaging — conversations are filtered (for example, sharing contact details before the family stage is blocked), which limits avenues for abuse.
  • Human moderation — reports are reviewed by our team, and accounts that violate this policy are removed.
  • No open/public content — Ikhlaas is not a public feed; there is no anonymous broadcast surface for abusive content.

4. Reporting child safety concerns

Anyone can report a child safety concern. Inside the app, use the Report option on a profile or conversation. You can also contact our child-safety point of contact directly:

Child-safety contact: [SAFETY EMAIL]

We aim to acknowledge child-safety reports promptly and act on confirmed violations without delay.

5. How we respond

  • We remove offending content and permanently ban the account(s) involved.
  • We preserve relevant data as required for investigation and legal reporting.
  • We report apparent CSAM to the appropriate authorities and hotlines — including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable, and relevant regional and national authorities (in India, this includes reporting to the designated law-enforcement and cyber-crime authorities).
  • We cooperate with lawful requests from law-enforcement agencies.

6. Compliance

Ikhlaas complies with applicable child-safety laws in the jurisdictions where it operates, and reports to regional and national authorities as required. Our designated point of contact is able to speak to our CSAM-prevention practices and compliance.
